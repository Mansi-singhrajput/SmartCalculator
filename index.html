LPG Safety & Precaution Guidelines

Follow standard statutory safety norms to keep your family and home protected.

👃

Gas Leakage Precautions

If you detect the pungent smell of Ethyl Mercaptan, do not switch electrical appliances ON or OFF. Extinguish all open flames and open windows immediately.

🛡️

Check Safety Seal & Tare Weight

Ensure the white plastic safety cap and hologram seal are intact upon delivery. Verify the tare weight stamped on cylinder stay-plate collar.

🔝

Always Keep Upright

Store cylinders vertically on a flat, firm surface at ground level. Never store cylinders horizontally or in confined basements.

🚨

Emergency Helpline: 1906

Dial national 24-hour toll-free emergency helpline 1906 for instant technical assistance and leak response team dispatch.
